Lift & Shift — Component Library

Reusable .ls-* components for building legacy desktop master/detail data-entry screens on the web, in a modern flat look (gray canvas, rounded white cards, hairline borders, compact fields). Built on the Contruent design system. Link lift-shift.css and follow the patterns below when creating a new lift & shift page.

1. Getting started

Load the four stylesheets (in order) plus components/script.js, then wrap your screen in .ls-screen. Each direct child becomes a rounded card with 12px gaps.

<!-- in <head> -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="../vendor/primeicons/primeicons.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="../components/tokens.css" />   <!-- tokens BEFORE styles -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="../components/styles.css" />
<link rel="stylesheet" href="lift-shift.css" />

<!-- screen shell -->
<div class="ls-screen">
  <div class="toolbar">…</div>                <!-- card -->
  <div class="ls-split">
    <section class="panel ls-nav">…</section>   <!-- master card -->
    <section class="panel ls-data">…</section>  <!-- detail card -->
  </div>
  <section class="panel ls-bottom">…</section>  <!-- card -->
</div>

<script src="../components/script.js"></script>   <!-- dropdowns / checkboxes / tabs -->

4. Panel header & master / detail split

Section titles are subtle uppercase gray labels (.ls-head › .panel-title) with a hairline underline. .ls-split lays out a fixed-width .ls-nav master card and a fluid .ls-data detail card.

10. Bottom detail panel & tab strip

A fixed-height .ls-bottom card whose tab strip acts as the header. Use the DS Tab Menu (.tab-menu › .tab-menu-item, active = .active) inside .ls-tabs-wrap, with an optional .ls-tabs-more scroll arrow; the body (.ls-tabbody) fills the rest and scrolls.
